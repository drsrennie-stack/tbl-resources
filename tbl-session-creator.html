      03

      Readiness assurance (iRAT, tRAT, appeals)

      The RAT checks that students came prepared with the concepts the application exercise will need.

      How the RAT is built. Questions sit at DOK 2 or DOK 3. Recall-only items (DOK 1) are rewritten, and full clinical decisions (DOK 4) are moved to the application exercise. Items follow the NBME single-best-answer rules, the tRAT uses immediate feedback, and teams can file written appeals afterward.

      04

      Application exercise and facilitation

      The application is designed first, and the RAT is built to prepare students for it.

      4S design. Every team works the same significant problem, commits to one specific choice, and reveals it at the same moment. The choice is written so that two well-prepared teams could land on different answers and defend them, which is what drives the whole-class discussion.

      06

      What this tool is built on

      The prompt applies these sources. Links open in a new tab.

      • Haidet P, Levine RE, Parmelee DX, et al. Guidelines for reporting team-based learning activities in the medical and health sciences education literature. Academic Medicine, 2012. Defines the seven core design elements used here.
      • Parmelee D, Michaelsen LK, Cook S, Hudes PD. Team-based learning: a practical guide. AMEE Guide No. 65. Medical Teacher, 2012.
      • Alizadeh M, et al. Team-based learning in health professions education: an umbrella review. BMC Medical Education, 2024. Found the application exercise and peer evaluation are the least reported elements.
      • Valter K, et al. The practices and strategies for implementing team-based learning in pre-clinical medical education: a systematic review. BMC Medical Education, 2026. Only 3 of 37 studies reported designing applications with the 4S framework.
      • NBME Item-Writing Guide, 6th edition. National Board of Medical Examiners. Single-best-answer structure and technical flaws to avoid.
      • Team-Based Learning Collaborative. teambasedlearning.org.
